Subject: Trouble verifying your location? Try this
---
Hi {{display_name}},

We noticed your location checks aren't passing. Here are some tips:

For Android:
1. Settings → Location → turn ON "Google Location Accuracy"
2. Settings → Apps → your browser → Permissions → Location → select "Precise"
3. Try again outdoors or near a window

For iPhone:
1. Settings → Privacy → Location Services → ON
2. Your browser → "While Using" + "Precise Location" ON
3. Try again outdoors or near a window

If location checks still don't work, you can try vouching instead — ask a friend on Konvo to verify you at app.thekonvo.com/vouch.

— Konvo Team
